# Extended jobs

QRSS, FSKCW, and DFCW message inputs accept at most 32 characters including spaces. The inherited Morse alphabet has a maximum of six elements per supported character (`?`, `.`, `,`, `-`). The worst 32-character message contains 192 marks, 160 intra-character gaps, and 31 inter-character gaps, for 383 events in every mode. The Pico compiler adds a one-microsecond final RF-off interval, so its worst single-message plan is 384 events. Leading and trailing whitespace are silent, and consecutive internal whitespace selects one inter-word gap. All whitespace counts toward the input limit.

Each native message job is bounded to 512 events and 3,600 seconds. The [message-schedule resource](../browser-api.md) saves mode, text, frequencies, timing, repeat settings, and recurrence. The Pico compiles each eligible occurrence from the saved settings before arming it. The browser previews the calculated event count, carrier frequencies, and duration.

Finite repetition is bounded expansion. The compiler computes duration and event count with checked arithmetic before allocating the expanded job. Repeats include inter-message gaps and the final RF-off interval in the total duration. Jobs over either limit are refused. The engine owns repeat and symbol timing locally.

The RF engine allocates a bounded segment vector during preparation and takes ownership of it. The two 16,384-word waveform buffers stay fixed throughout execution. Disable releases the prepared plan after the sink confirms stop. Duration and sample arithmetic use 64-bit values, including 496,800,000,000 samples per hour at 138 MHz. Peak heap, preparation latency, and refill deadlines require target validation for any new configuration. See [RF stream](rf-stream.md) for buffer ownership and failure handling.

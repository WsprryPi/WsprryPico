# Si5351 engine and reference measurement

The application selects either direct GPIO/PIO generation or the Si5351 engine from the immutable accepted boot allocation. Si5351 controls, persistence, portable planning, local execution and reference measurement use the existing JobService and RF worker. Ordinary builds refuse Si5351 transmission and reference-output activation. The explicit `WsprryPico-Si5351Engineering` target (or `WSPRRY_PICO_SI5351_ENGINEERING=ON` in a private build) enables physically unqualified execution for a separately verified conducted test setup. GPS/PPS ingestion and automatic RF calibration are unavailable.

## Planning

`src/rf/si5351_plan.*` constructs fixed-size plans for at most four distinct tones. The plan binds the complete synthesis configuration, manual correction, requested frequencies, quantization budget, achieved frequencies, and divider/register representations. Device configuration rebuilds and checks that plan before bus writes.

PLLA remains fixed across transmit tones. A selected loopback uses PLLB and a separate nominal 1 MHz divider plan unaffected by applied transmit correction. None uses an absent output selection; CLK0 is a real output. Positive correction means a fast source: the programmed transmit frequency is divided by `1 + correction_ppb / 1e9` once. Correction is bounded to the existing ±100,000 ppb range. These calculations establish no physical carrier accuracy.

`src/rf/si5351_job.*` compiles complete `rf-events/1` jobs into fixed storage of at most 4 KiB, with 512 event ends, four distinct tones, an immutable job digest, and the existing one-hour limit. Absolute event boundaries round independently to microseconds. The caller must supply a qualified minimum transition interval. Unsupported frequencies, excess tones, malformed timelines, and missing frequency-adjustment consent are refused without hardware access. The engine consumes this immutable plan locally, with a minimum 10 ms event interval. It admits WSPR, finite tone, QRSS, FSKCW and DFCW only when the complete plan meets these limits. This numerical admission does not qualify physical modulation or timing.

The numerical implementation covers 25 MHz and 27 MHz references and CLK0–CLK2. It uses a nominal 899 MHz PLL, leaves margin below the VCO ceiling, and keeps the MultiSynth output at least 1 MHz before the R divider. Special divide-by-4/divide-by-6 plans and PLL-retuned modulation are refused. Low-frequency and upper-frequency admission follows the complete corrected plan, rather than a band label. Synthesis feasibility does not qualify any mode or transition.

## Registers and resource ownership

The supported register model is Si5351A 10-MSOP with standard address `0x60`. An ACK does not identify the silicon or establish module compatibility. Other packages, custom addressing, and substitutes require separate support. Passive crystals require an explicit 6, 8, or 10 pF load. Register 183 uses the documented reserved-bit encoding. External XA restores the documented 10 pF reset selection rather than retaining a previous crystal setting; it is never translated into CLKIN selection. The selected external module is the QRP Labs Rev. 5 board with its 25 MHz TCXO, installed according to its assembly manual without the supplied crystal and C3. Other external-XA arrangements require the datasheet's coupling, amplitude, and floating-XB requirements and independent qualification. Register settings do not prove that wiring or input levels meet those requirements.

`src/hardware/si5351_settings.*` validates one approved I²C pair, distinct transmit/reference outputs, optional loopback input, optional external gate, and existing GPIO owners. PWM counting requires an eligible B-channel input and reserves its entire slice independently of GPIO ownership. GP7 maps to slice 3 B; GP6/GP7 cannot simultaneously serve as the I²C pair. No physical wiring or gate presence follows from accepted allocation.

`Device` distinguishes unknown output state from register-disabled and register-enabled observations. Initialization masks outputs and powers down all three supported outputs before programming. Readback failure, NACK, and partial writes retain unknown state. PLLA and PLLB lock observations remain separate. No output can be enabled before the applicable lock observation. Quiescence attempts both mask and powerdown control. None of these observations measures physical RF inactivity.

`PicoBus` is an RF-owner adapter with no constructor side effects. It uses the approved hardware I²C controller at 100 kHz, recomputes the remaining absolute deadline between transfer phases, and caps individual transfer budgets at 5 ms. Failure closes the controller; recovery requires owner-controlled reopening. No clock output is enabled by opening the bus.

## Reference measurements

Reference windows contain extended edge counts, raw monotonic endpoints, capture uncertainty, and hardware-plan generation. The bounded 16-bit counter extension rejects service gaps that could hide multiple wraps using a hardware edge-rate bound, independently of the nominal input frequency. Coherent snapshots and endpoint brackets participate in the uncertainty calculation. Plan changes invalidate a window.

`PicoReferenceCapture` implements input-only PWM sampling with exclusive slice ownership, outward-rounded timestamp brackets, a conservative hardware edge-rate bound, and clock-tree-change invalidation. It requires frequent owner polling and rejects starvation. It is instantiated only for the selected Si5351 allocation. The physical setup and service budgets remain unqualified.

Measurement-only policy produces a ratio and uncertainty. Trusted-local policy additionally requires an explicit nonzero oscillator uncertainty allowance and can calculate a local rate estimate. Neither supplies a UTC epoch or absolute RF calibration. The application passes timestamped trusted-local rate observations to the existing μChrony engine, at most once per second, with their original sample time and expiry. This assists only μChrony-derived UTC; browser/controller epoch observations retain their existing policy. A rate observation cannot establish UTC, refresh an NTP sample age, change raw hardware time, or correct RF. Independent rate disagreement blocks UTC admission across all epoch sources. Missing or stale capture retires freshness, retains the learned rate center with conservative ordinary holdover, and cannot clear a known disagreement. Explicit policy removal or a new independent agreeing observation is required. Freshness checks never refresh observation age.

## Validation boundary

The registered `si5351_plan`, `si5351_job`, `si5351_allocation`, `si5351_device`, `reference_measurement`, and `reference_counter` tests exercise arithmetic, complete timelines, refusal before writes, failed/partial control, lock separation, resource conflicts, rollover ambiguity, plan binding, and uncertainty. `pico_reference_capture` compiles the actual capture adapter against a behavioral SDK fixture that checks input direction through every GPIO handover, slice ownership, counting, starvation, and clock changes. Engine, monitor, settings, schema and shared browser tests also exercise complete execution, bounded shutdown, interrupted windows, current closed settings, refusal before persistence, None/CLK0 and restart latching. These are deterministic software checks. Both actual companion network and USB production pair suites are required for the intended sources.

Physical qualification remains selected unfinished work in the [consolidated backlog](consolidated-backlog.md). No register readback or software test proves physical RF inactivity. A separately powered generator can continue through Pico reset or an I²C failure; an optional independently biased RF gate is a distinct resource, not an inferred feature of the module.

## Configuration and observations

RF Output offers Direct GPIO or Si5351. Selecting an engine changes only the browser draft. Si5351 requires an explicit approved SDA/SCL pair, fitted reference frequency/source and transmit output. Crystal load is visible only for passive crystals. Loopback is None or a distinct CLK0/1/2 with an eligible input GPIO. None releases capture resources and dependent trusted use. Reference use defaults to measurement only; trusted use requires a declared uncertainty. Drive is 2/4/6/8 mA and is independent of reported WSPR dBm. Manual correction is engine-specific and survives engine changes without transferring between oscillators.

The current Control/1 config accepts optional `si5351` settings. Engine activation uses one atomic `config/update` merge containing the complete `pins` and `si5351` allocation, checked together with separate filter resources before writes. Inactive saved Si5351 settings own no GPIOs. Hardware/reference changes latch restart and output inhibition, including A/B/A saves; the boot plan never changes in place. Station, network, STOP and existing journal size limits remain independent.

GET `/api/control/v1/si5351` exposes saved settings, boot-active settings, restart state, engineering gate and nullable runtime evidence. Communication means verified register control or unknown, not silicon identification. Transmit and reference register states are independent. Measurements report raw endpoints, counts, endpoint uncertainty, ratio, age, plan generation and reference-use policy. Samples older than 30 seconds are omitted. Clock-source status distinguishes selected, lower-quality, awaiting UTC, expired and disagreement. The same resource is available through authenticated Pi management over USB, engineering TLS and Consumer-Radio.

## Execution and electrical limits

All preparation, symbol-time I²C, output control and cleanup belong to the RF owner. Background measurement pauses before a job and interrupted windows are discarded. Finite reference windows can resume only when the engine is idle and TX registers are verified disabled, including after verified cleanup of a failed or missed job. The terminal job remains failed or missed; reference acquisition does not clear its ownership or restart it. Unknown TX state blocks acquisition. Permanent configuration or safety inhibition suppresses reference restart. PLLA remains stable during continuous modulation; PLLB supplies the independent loopback. No per-symbol output inhibition or PLL reset is introduced for continuous modes. Amplifier/filter lead, settle and tail intervals remain local and complete; failed control retains unknown output state and prevents terminal success.

Direct PIO/DMA retains its 100 µs autonomous hardware-acknowledgement limit. Si5351 uses an explicitly separate external-control completion path, bounded to 8 ms and capped independently by the existing advertised WTP output-disable timeout. Final output-off is commanded at the final event boundary; successful ACK remains unknown until register verification. JobService must confirm shutdown before terminal success. This is output-control latency, not permission to claim sample-accurate RF cutoff or measured timing. A single register write needs 27 I²C clocks (270 µs at 100 kHz); complete keyed retune/prime operations need several writes and readbacks. The 8 ms operation budget fits inside the admitted 10 ms minimum event interval. Transfer deadlines, missed-boundary refusal and unknown-state handling remain enforced.

The adapter uses 100 kHz and enables Pico internal pull-ups, matching the documented bench baseline. A faster bus or a wiring change is not a prerequisite. Electrical levels, rise time, transfer reliability, physical transition latency and failed-bus/reset containment still require measurement. An independent gate can provide a separate physical cutoff when explicitly wired and verified; none is inferred from accepted settings. Software ACK timestamps do not establish physical RF latency.

Reset, power interruption, stuck bus, failed shutdown, physical transition latency and mode waveforms require separately recorded hardware qualification. Ordinary builds keep these unqualified output capabilities disabled.

## Source provenance

This is original MIT implementation using mathematical/register facts from [Skyworks AN619 revision 0.8](https://www.skyworksinc.com/-/media/SkyWorks/SL/documents/public/application-notes/AN619.pdf) and the [Si5351 revision 1.3 datasheet](https://www.skyworksinc.com/-/media/SkyWorks/SL/documents/public/data-sheets/Si5351-B.pdf). The supported-module decision must also account for the manufacturer's [QRP Labs revision 5 assembly manual](https://qrp-labs.com/images/synth/synth_assembly5.pdf). The approved GPIO mapping follows the pinned Pico SDK and the [RP2350 datasheet](https://datasheets.raspberrypi.com/rp2350/rp2350-datasheet.pdf). No manufacturer's example driver is incorporated.

Engineering deployment uses `usb_installer.py plan --local-build … --local-variant si5351-engineering` and the resulting retained bundle through the normal preserving upgrade transaction. Direct programming is not a substitute. See the [developer installer workflow](usb-installer.md#explicit-developer-builds).

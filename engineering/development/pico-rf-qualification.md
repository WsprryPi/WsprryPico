# Pico RF qualification

Current firmware executes complete jobs locally through its native scheduler and [authenticated finite Test Tone](../protocol/Native-Execution.md#authenticated-finite-test-tone) control. It does not accept external WTP jobs over USB, TCP, TLS, BLE, or HTTP. The [Harness WTP campaign workflow](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi-Qualification-Harness/blob/devel/docs/development/pico-rf-campaign.md) describes the older transport and cannot run that workflow against current firmware. Its historical results remain bound to their original source pair, image, and evidence format.

For current conducted observations, configure WSPR and keyed messages through the device's ordinary native controls. Use authenticated Test Tone Review, Start, identified Status, and exact-job Stop for finite steady carriers. Capture and analyze RF with separately identified receiver tooling. These controls do not provide arbitrary event upload or a firmware-side batch campaign. Select the actual controller, firmware, and analysis sources before acquisition; a branch name alone does not identify a tested pair.

## Campaign coverage

A campaign manifest fixes board and module identity, exact firmware/bundle hashes, RF engine and output, clock, reference and correction policy, drive, requested and realized carriers, modes, repetitions, receiver, and conducted path. GPS-disabled, GPS-enabled/manual, and GPS-enabled/automatic cases are separate observations when selected. GPS-enabled cases need usable serial/PPS association; physically disconnecting a receiver is not required merely to select manual correction. The [GPS/PPS contract](gps-pps.md) defines correction admission and frozen job provenance.

Mode qualification uses three independent complete observations per selected point, mode, and drive. Three on/off cycles inside one job are one observation. WSPR acceptance requires the exact expected external `wsprd` decode and the separately selected finite carrier gate. QRSS, FSKCW, and DFCW use the maintained complete marks, gaps, and frequency-state criteria in the [Si5351 mode acceptance guide](si5351.md#conducted-mode-acceptance). Freeze thresholds, capture windows, analyzer identities, receiver noise limits, and timing allowances before acquisition. Do not relax them after seeing results. Record the supported WSPR message types for the selected decoder rather than assuming that Type 1 acceptance covers paired Type 2/3 messages.

### Four-drive Si5351 qualification

The ordinary `qrp-labs-rev6-25mhz-clk0` profile admits 2, 4, 6, and 8 mA on the QRP Labs Rev. 6 / 25 MHz external-XA TCXO / CLK0 setup. The retained [17-point, five-mode matrix](si5351-band-support.json) records its original 8 mA engineering image and captures. Qualification of that matrix at all four drives requires 340 cells with three independent observations per cell: 1,020 observations, plus the selected WSPR carrier gates. These are coverage requirements, not a claim that the expanded matrix has passed. The [HP power sweeps](../../docs/reference/output.md) measure sensor-input power at their own recorded carriers and drives; they do not establish that mode matrix, calibrated fundamental power, or shutdown timing.

Group acquisitions by drive using ordinary save, explicit restart, rediscovery, and authoritative boot readback. Test Tone never programs a per-job drive. Both native schedule types must be disabled for finite tones, including suspended-but-enabled schedules. Use existing commissioning and configuration authority, preserve an absent station, and never invent credentials or a special bench configuration. Abort the group on changed identity, unverified drive, or unresolved shutdown. Do not retry an uncertain Start.

For each drive, lifecycle observations include the minimum admitted duration, natural completion, explicit Stop, and a maximum-duration tone that finishes without caller renewal after the native lease expires. These can count toward the selected tone repetitions when they meet the same evidence requirements. Capture quiet before RF, the accepted running interval, and quiet after termination. Compare duration and cutoff against the frozen software budgets plus an independently stated receiver timing allowance. Register readback, elapsed time, or an HTTP success alone does not prove physical carrier disappearance.

The profile has no independent RF gate. A separately powered Si5351 can continue through a stuck I²C bus or Pico reset. Deterministic fault injection covers failed writes, readback, lock, timeout, and bounded recovery behavior; live electrical fault injection requires a separately selected safe setup. Unknown shutdown remains unresolved and blocks another acquisition or successful handback. A software timeout is not an unconditional hardware cutoff guarantee.

## Setup and execution boundaries

Verify the current board, USB parent and management role, output pin or module connector, receiver identity, termination, attenuation, filters, combiner routing, antenna disconnection, and safe receiver-input basis before device access. Historical wiring records are not evidence of the present setup. Record original settings, disabled recurrence, time and correction state, and a supported handback path before deployment. Current setup authorization does not establish measurement accuracy.

Deploy ordinary firmware through the [shared installer](usb-installer.md) and its exact retained developer bundle, preserving the same-board installed-image reference and postboot verification. Do not substitute direct `picotool load`, edit identities, or delete installer references. A stale reference is reconciled only with its original bundle and exact same-board flash comparison through `upgrade --installed-bundle`. RAM-only diagnostics retain their [full-flash comparison and restoration duties](../../tests/hardware/si5351-diagnostics.md); their observations cannot replace ordinary-candidate qualification.

Read and retain original settings before acquisitions. After every drive restart, verify the new boot, current capability, saved and boot-active drive, storage health, disabled schedules, and idle/unowned output. Review and Start one absolute carrier at a time. Verify job-bound pad or synthesis evidence and fit meter settling/readout inside the observed running interval, allowing transport age and an explicit Stop margin. Reauthenticate and read the same request/job after a lost reply; missing receipts never prove that nothing ran. Confirm native cleanup and independent physical RF inactivity before another point or settings save.

At handback, restore the agreed original image/settings through supported installer and configuration operations. Verify image/reference identity, saved and boot settings, released ownership, disabled test recurrence, and physical RF inactivity. Preserve the exact original and candidate bundles, settings, and recovery records until handback is verified. Restoring a previously unsaved default can persist that setting; disclose the difference. Do not silently re-enable recurring RF schedules. An unsupported downgrade does not authorize erase, factory reset, or fresh-default handback; agree on the specific supported alternative before deployment. Failed restoration or unknown output blocks successful handback.

## Exact-artifact acceptance

Prepare firmware through the [canonical build](software-build-ci.md) and [retained publication](prepared-publication.md) workflows with publication disabled. Retain canonical deployment/audit archives, developer bundle, source and dirty-input identity, dependency/toolchain revisions, version, run/attempt, manifests, and payload hashes. Preview images and renamed CI artifacts do not establish a release candidate. Test the exact final ordinary artifact; later firmware-input changes require an impact assessment and repetition of affected physical observations.

Installation acceptance distinguishes independently verified fresh installation from preservation of a compatible identified baseline. Preserve evidence for defaults, identical-image reinstall, upgrade, migration, settings round trips, and supported recovery. Hostname-only checks do not prove full disabled-configuration preservation. Historical [Harness lifecycle procedures](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi-Qualification-Harness/blob/devel/docs/development/lifecycle-release.md) require source/interface compatibility review before reuse; they do not restore retired job transports.

Check the installed browser at its current IP and resolved hostname using independent fresh sessions, with recurrence disabled and output inactive. Cover authentication, lock feedback, desktop/mobile layout, saved settings, restart behavior, and A/B/A hardware-save inhibition. Retain failed attempts and their reconciled outcomes alongside passing observations.

## Evidence and claims

Keep private raw IQ, transcripts, flash backups, and run bundles outside Git. Retain the frozen manifest, original and failed observations, actual control transactions and transport ACKs where applicable, job/device/boot bindings, requested and realized carriers, correction snapshots, pad/register verification basis, receiver settings, exact-count capture metadata, decoder output, analysis, and final cleanup/settings state. Choose validators for the actual evidence format; the older WTP `validate-complete-test` format does not validate a native-control campaign automatically. Neither compiler identity nor a source revision alone identifies the installed bytes.

The [RF calibration diagnostic schema](../protocol/rf-calibration-diagnostics.schema.json) retains frozen automatic-correction inputs separately from later live estimates. The [network diagnostic schema](../protocol/network-diagnostics.schema.json) defines platform observations independently of the shared control schema. Validate complete shape and exact bindings before interpreting evidence; do not combine source/boot cohorts or replace unavailable observations with defaults.

Shared capability or protocol changes require both actual production network and USB source-pair suites for the intended Pico/Pi candidates under the [CI execution contract](software-build-ci.md#build-reuse-and-source-pairs). Record revisions, tested uncommitted inputs, exact commands/results, and source-bound CI receipts. Missing, skipped, cancelled, timed-out, failed, stale, or wrong-source results do not satisfy that gate. These software suites do not restore external WTP execution or prove physical USB, installation, timing, or RF performance. Unresolved integration and physical qualification requirements remain open until their identified evidence passes.

Conducted results apply only to the recorded board, module/reference, firmware artifact, engine, drive, mode, carrier, clock/correction state, receiver, and path. Decode success proves neither spectral compliance nor calibrated power or absolute UTC accuracy. The [GPS/PPS investigation disposition](gps-pps-validation.md#bounded-investigation-closure-2026-11-08) remains closed as inconclusive and resolution-limited; automatic correction is available for experimentation without a requirement that it improve accuracy or justify wider acquisition windows.

The following RC.2 record preserves the original candidate installation, browser, RF observations, failed attempts, and unresolved handback scope. It is historical evidence, not the current board state or an instruction to resume a campaign. The [publication guide](prepared-publication.md#rc2-published-artifact-provenance) records subsequent publication; publication does not complete missing physical acceptance.

NTP or authenticated phone time supplies the epoch and phase observations; Si5351 loopback supplies an independently bounded frequency reference between observations. GPS is an optional later enhancement. Loopback alone cannot establish UTC, authenticate network time, remove initial phase error, or calibrate RF automatically.

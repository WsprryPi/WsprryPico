# CI reliability and efficiency scorecard

These measurements describe the source revisions and observation sets identified below and in the [accounting ledger](accounting.json). Coverage, strict refusal, owned cleanup, and independent defect detection remain acceptance requirements; speed and size reductions do not justify skipped work or wider product timing. Current commands and source-bound completion gates are defined in the [CI execution contract](../software-build-ci.md#execution-completeness-and-local-qualification).

The current operating instructions are in the [CI guide](../software-build-ci.md). The [accounting ledger](accounting.json) contains exact baseline/current scoped paths, byte/physical-line counts and hashes, companion costs, registration reconciliation, observation denominators, qualified unchanged inputs, and exact tracked retirement inventories. It is comparison data for this requested scorecard, not an archive of completed prompts or intermediate reports.

## Observations and unsuccessful attempts

Final adversarial assessment reproduced actual SIGTERM arriving after child exit but during result qualification being ignored, with a successful return and passed receipt. The green earlier candidate `6c1d6fcb…` and its canonical run [42133077033](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPico/actions/runs/42133077033) therefore remain superseded evidence, not final qualification. Cancellation now propagates through qualification, receipt commit, and signal-handler handoff; failed receipts are rewritten atomically when possible. Pending cancellation and qualification/log/storage failures remain visible together. A failed child and log flush/close or handler-restoration failure retain both causes.

The macOS actual-READY probe also exposed Python 3.15.2 kqueue wait recovery catching the old InterruptedError as OSError and continuing to wait. Cancellation now has a distinct non-I/O exception and escapes that recovery path. The original READY probe passes with its unchanged guard. Seven added methods bring the execution suite from 24 to 31; all pass on macOS and Linux. Three private old-source counterfactuals fail the intended actual-signal rejection assertions. No arbitrary sleep, timeout expansion, or product timing change was introduced. Corrected committed-head CI, immutable artifacts, and both actual source-pair recipes must pass again; a prior green candidate is not relabelled as the correction.

The accepted matched scheduling window binds Pico `4117a37ce8891d02a75029f112e6ddab08ba60d9` / Pi `28915d31026b2c9a97f451c01d48820fb52c097f`, unchanged case inventory and seed, workers 1/2/2/1, and the same warmed 18-CPU Docker environment. It is not a cold-cache, constrained-runner, final-source, or general flakiness claim. The earlier A/B/B/A window failed its closing serial run with HOST_DRY_RUN_MISSED; that entire window was invalidated. Narrow UTC launch windows and torn clock correlation were reproduced deterministically and repaired in the test fixture without widening product policy. Do not filter the failed observation.

The measured Pico PR observation set contains fifteen distinct candidate runs: twelve successful, three cancelled, all attempt 1. They total 14,876 verify-job seconds plus 47 seconds from run creation to verify start, across a 27,308-second elapsed observation window. These are different sources/coverage/runner states with engineering interventions, not a same-source reliability sample or total project cost. Cancelled runs are 42062323882, 42062819269, and 42105319549; the last was an intentionally superseded build candidate, and none is successful verification. No precise causal classification is asserted for the first two cancellations.

Other unsuccessful results remain material: an early selected-server mismatch, local fixed-port overlap, Mac-backed credential permissions, discarded empty CTest selection, unentered/wrong-failure mutation probes, the failed scheduling window, a Pi working-directory worker failure, and a USB lane cancelled after redundant APT downloads reached its unchanged job budget. Corrected sources or orchestration were validated afterward; a later green run does not erase these outcomes. A targeted USB rerun succeeded but its whole Pi run retained an unrelated cancelled release job and was not whole-CI success. The later exact pair [42123604429](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/actions/runs/42123604429) passed all nine jobs.

# CI reliability and efficiency scorecard

Reliability, complete coverage, strict refusal, safe ownership, and independent
defect detection govern this campaign. Speed and reduction targets did not remove
required work or widen product timing. The implementation and substantive review
are complete; delivery requires the exact final Git head's successful hosted
verification, both production source-pair recipes, and independent artifact/source
rebinding. Those final run identities accompany the delivery report and the
[PR's current checks](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPico/pull/16/checks).
A failed, skipped, missing, cancelled, or wrong-source required result reopens
acceptance. Optional publication is intentionally skipped.

The current operating instructions are in the [CI guide](../software-build-ci.md).
The [accounting ledger](accounting.json) contains exact baseline/current scoped
paths, byte/physical-line counts and hashes, companion costs, registration
reconciliation, observation denominators, qualified unchanged inputs, and exact
tracked retirement inventories. It is comparison data for this requested
scorecard, not an archive of completed prompts or intermediate reports.
